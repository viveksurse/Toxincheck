Ingredient Label Scanner

Capture a product label, extract its ingredient text, and review the information before analysis.

1. Scan the product label

Place the ingredient list in good light. Keep the label steady and make sure the text is readable.

Your camera preview will appear here.

Open the camera or upload a photo of the label.

2. Review the ingredients

Check the extracted text carefully. Camera images can be unclear, so correct any mistakes before continuing.

Safety note: Ingredient lists alone cannot establish whether a product is safe or harmful. Risk can depend on concentration, intended use, and exposure. This scanner provides educational information, not a medical diagnosis.
